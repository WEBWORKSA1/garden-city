Common questions

Is Garden.City really free?

Yes. All guides and tools are free. We’re supported by advertising, sponsorships, reader donations and a referral fee some garden professionals pay when they receive a project request.

How does quote matching work?

You describe your project once. We share it with up to three suitable local professionals who contact you with quotes. You’re never obliged to hire anyone.

Can I grow food with only a few hours of sun?

Yes — leafy greens, herbs like mint and parsley, and many Asian greens produce well with 3–4 hours. Use the Plant Finder’s “part shade” filter.

Is Garden.City connected to a place called Garden City?

No. Garden.City is an independent publication. See our trademark notice.
