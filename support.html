Where your money goes

We publish how support is used. Our target allocation:

Operations — hosting, tools, software (30%)


Hiring talent — writers, horticulturists, video creators (30%)


Contests & prizes (20%)


Promotion & marketing — reaching new gardeners and community groups (20%)

Please note: Garden.City is an independent publisher, not a registered charity. Contributions are not tax-deductible. Contributors receive a thank-you note and, optionally, a credit on our supporters page.
