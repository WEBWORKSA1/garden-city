The City Garden Starter Kit

Everything you need for a successful first season on a balcony, rooftop or windowsill. Print it, pin it up and tick it off.

1. Setup checklist

  • Read your building / lease rules on planters, railings and water runoff.
  • Map your light for one full sunny day (worksheet below).
  • Estimate weight with the load calculator — heavy pots go near walls.
  • Find your last spring frost date and open the Planting Calendar.
  • Choose 5–8 crops with the Plant Finder.
  • Buy containers with drainage holes, saucers and quality potting mix.
  • Set up a watering routine: a can within reach, or a drip kit on a timer.
  • Start a garden notebook: what you planted, when, and what happened.

6+ hours of sun = full sun crops. 3–6 hours = greens, herbs, roots. Under 3 = mint, microgreens, ferns.

3. Shopping list

  • Containers: 2 × 30 L (fruiting crops), 2 × troughs 60–80 cm (greens/herbs), 1 hanging basket
  • Potting mix (calculate litres first)
  • Slow-release fertiliser + liquid feed
  • Seeds or seedlings for your chosen crops
  • Stakes, trellis or strings for climbers
  • Watering can or drip kit, saucers, a pair of snips

5. Weekly routine

  • Daily (summer): finger-test soil, water deeply when dry.
  • Weekly: harvest, pinch herbs, check leaf undersides for pests, rotate pots.
  • Every 2 weeks: liquid-feed fruiting crops.
  • Monthly: sow a new batch of quick greens.
