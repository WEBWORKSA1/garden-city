Quote FAQ

How much does a balcony garden makeover cost?

Simple container plantings with quality pots and soil often run a few hundred dollars; designed makeovers with built-in planters, irrigation, lighting and furniture can reach several thousand. Weight limits and building rules affect the price.

Do I need permission for a rooftop garden?

Usually. Condo boards, landlords and many cities require approval and sometimes a structural engineer’s sign-off. Good rooftop contractors handle this — ask for it in your quote.

Will I get spam calls?

No. Your request goes only to a maximum of three matched professionals for this project. We do not sell lists.

Is there a fee?

Not for you. Some professionals pay Garden.City a referral fee when they receive a project, which keeps the service free for homeowners.
