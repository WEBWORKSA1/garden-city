Official rules (summary)

Eligibility

Open to individuals aged 18+ where such contests are lawful. Void where prohibited or restricted. Employees and contractors of Garden.City and sponsors, and their households, are not eligible.

No purchase necessary

Entry is free. Donations, purchases or sponsorships do not improve your chances of winning.

Your photos

You keep copyright. By entering you grant Garden.City a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display your photos and first name/city on the website, newsletter and social channels in connection with the contest, with credit. Do not submit photos showing identifiable people without their consent.

Judging and voting

A panel scores finalists using the published criteria; the People’s Choice is decided by public vote with fraud checks. Decisions are final.

Prizes

Prizes are non-transferable and have no cash alternative unless stated. Winners may need to confirm eligibility and may be responsible for applicable taxes. Unclaimed prizes after 30 days may be awarded to a runner-up.

Privacy

We use entry details only to run the contest and contact you. See our Privacy Policy.
