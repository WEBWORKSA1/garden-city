Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026.

This policy explains what information Garden.City (“we”, “us”) collects when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit a form — quote requests, newsletter sign-ups, contest registrations, donation pledges, job applications, pro applications or contact messages — we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, location and project information. Form submissions are delivered to us through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-processing service, which processes the data to deliver it.

Quote requests

If you request quotes, you consent to us sharing your project details and contact information with up to three garden professionals we believe can help. Those professionals may contact you about your request. We do not sell your personal information to data brokers or list sellers.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and the third-party services below may collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed and referring pages.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info and, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message, and personalised ads are only served with consent. Choosing “Essential only” in our cookie notice requests non-personalised ads.

Browser storage

Our tools save your preferences (for example your frost date or garden plan) in your browser’s local storage so they are there when you return. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Once played, YouTube (Google) may collect data under its own privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

How we use information

  • To respond to messages and fulfil requests (quotes, kits, contest entries, pledges).
  • To send newsletters you signed up for — every email includes an unsubscribe option.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose they were collected, or as required by law, and then delete them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada’s PIPEDA or California’s CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. To make a request, use our contact form and choose “Privacy request”.

Children

This website is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use our contact page.
